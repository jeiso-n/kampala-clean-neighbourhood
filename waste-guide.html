Waste education

Separate waste at source

Organic waste

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, and garden waste. Keep separate from plastics, glass, and hazardous materials. Suitable organic material may be composted where safe facilities and practices are available.

Recyclables

Clean, dry paper, cardboard, metal cans, and accepted plastics. Keep materials clean and check what local collection or recycling operators actually accept.

Residual waste

Items that cannot be reused, composted, or accepted for recycling locally. Store safely and use the approved collection arrangement for your area.

Do not mix batteries, chemicals, medical waste, or other hazardous items with ordinary household waste. Seek appropriate local guidance for safe handling.
