Privacy, Data Ethics & Reporting Guidance

How we handle information, respect citizen privacy, and promote fair reporting across Kampala's communities.

1. Static Client-Side Data Architecture

Kampala Clean Neighbourhood is built using client-side web technologies (HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript). The website does not transmit your personal contact details, IP address, or uploaded images to external third-party servers. All demonstration incident reports remain safely in your browser's private localStorage sandbox.

2. Anonymous Reporting

We recognize that reporting illegal dumping or commercial pollution can raise community safety concerns. Residents may submit reports without providing a name or telephone number.

3. Responsible Photo Evidence Guidelines

When photographing sanitation issues:

  • Focus directly on the environmental hazard (overflowing skip, blocked culvert, or dumped refuse).
  • Do not photograph children or identifiable bystanders without express consent.
  • Do not enter private properties to obtain photographs. Focus on public roads, road reserves, and drainage paths.

4. Academic & Non-Governmental Status

This platform is an educational prototype developed at Victoria University Kampala. Submitting a report here does not initiate official legal proceedings or municipal enforcement action under the National Environment Act.
