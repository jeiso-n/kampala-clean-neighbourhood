Digital ethics

Privacy and responsible reporting

What this prototype stores

The current incident form stores report text in the browser's localStorage on the device used to submit it. It does not upload reports to a server or notify KCCA, a local leader, or a waste collector. Browser data may be lost when site data is cleared.

Photos and personal information

Do not upload images containing faces, vehicle registration numbers, private documents, or other identifying details unless necessary and you have a lawful reason to share them. Avoid including sensitive personal information in descriptions.

Review and verification

Before any future public sharing, reports should be reviewed by an authorised administrator, checked against pilot boundaries, and assessed for authenticity. Never publish a reporter's contact details or exact home location.

Limitations

This prototype is not an emergency service or official KCCA reporting channel. For urgent hazards, contact the appropriate official service directly.
