Circular economy

Reuse, repair, recycle, compost

Composting

Where space and conditions allow, suitable food and garden waste can be composted. Keep meat, oils, plastics, and hazardous items out of a basic home compost system.

Plastic upcycling

Prioritise reducing and reusing packaging. Only clean and suitable plastics should be passed to a recycler or upcycling initiative that accepts them.
