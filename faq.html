Frequently asked questions

Does this website collect my rubbish?

No. It is an information and community reporting project, not a waste collection operator.

Are the schedules official?

Only schedules confirmed by the relevant provider or authority should be labelled verified. Always reconfirm service arrangements directly.

Does a report guarantee collection?

No. A report documents a concern; it does not guarantee an immediate response or collection.

Who is responsible for household waste?

Responsibilities and service arrangements depend on applicable laws, local authority guidance, property arrangements, and the provider. Seek current official guidance for your area.

Are reports sent to KCCA?

In this initial static version, reports are saved locally in the browser only and are not transmitted to KCCA or service providers.
