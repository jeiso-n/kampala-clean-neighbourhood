About the project

Better local waste information, together.

Kampala Clean Neighbourhood is a proposed community website for sharing verified household waste collection information, helping residents understand waste separation, and documenting local service concerns in selected Kampala communities.

The problem

Residents and small businesses can face unclear collection arrangements, delayed collection, and uncertainty about responsible disposal. The project focuses on practical local information rather than claiming to operate a city-wide collection service.
