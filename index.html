The Waste Management Challenge in Kampala

Kampala faces rapid urbanisation. Uncollected waste leads to blocked drainage channels, flash flooding in low-lying informal settlements, public health vulnerabilities, and environmental degradation in the Lake Victoria basin.

Uncertain Collection Times

Residents frequently miss collection trucks due to erratic scheduling, leaving waste exposed to scavengers and heavy rains on road reserves.

Check Division Schedules →

Collector Uncertainty

Difficulty distinguishing between certified private concessionaires and informal tippers who may illegally dump refuse in wetland buffer zones.

Browse Collector Directory →

Low Separation at Source

Mixed waste prevents high-value plastic recycling and household composting, overburdening transit transfer stations and municipal landfills.

How Kampala Clean Neighbourhood Works

Discover Schedules

Report & Mobilise

Report missed pickups, overflowing roadside skips, or illegal wetland dumping to create a public record for community action.
